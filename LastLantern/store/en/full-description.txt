The world has fallen into an endless night. You carry the last lantern.

Last Lantern is a one-thumb survival roguelite with hand-crafted pixel art. Each run is a single night: ten minutes of monsters pouring out of the dark. Your weapons fire on their own. You move, collect glowing embers, grow stronger, and try to see the sunrise.

YOUR LIGHT IS YOUR WEAPON
Enemies hide in the darkness. Only their eyes give them away until they step into your lantern's light. Lantern Oil widens the light and lets you hit what you can see.

A NEW BUILD EVERY NIGHT
• 8 weapons: Lantern Spark, Moth Swarm, Ember Ring, Chain Lightning, Bell Wave, Daggers, Flame Trail and Sickle
• 8 evolutions: max out a weapon, find its partner power, and open a chest to turn it into something legendary
• 10 passive powers to shape your hero

FACE THE DAWN
• 3 nights to conquer: the Whispering Woods, the Drowned Village and the Frozen Peak
• A great foe in the middle of every night and a dawn boss at the end of it
• Endless mode for players who never want the sun to rise

GROW STRONGER
• 4 heroes, each with a starting weapon and play style
• 14 permanent camp upgrades
• 30 achievements that reward gold and unlock heroes

MADE FOR PHONES
• Portrait mode, played with one thumb
• Short and intense runs, perfect for a break
• Fully playable offline
• No forced ads. Rewarded ads are optional: watch one only if you want to revive once or double your gold.
• Available in English and Turkish

Light the lantern. Survive the night.
